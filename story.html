Chapter 01 · Place, people and purpose

The Farm

A cultivated oasis in Upper Ojai, shaped by more than three decades of community life.

2022design snapshot

A place with a long memory

Full Circle Farm lies about six miles east of Ojai, where the broad valley narrows beneath the Los Padres mountains. Lion Creek follows the lower southern edge. Homes, gardens and workshops occupy the gentler ground above it.

The site was not a blank canvas. By 2022 it already held mature fruit trees, a productive garden, cabins, workshops, trailers, an earth dome, water tanks, animal systems and gathering spaces. The design therefore began with respect for what was already working.

Community at the center

The main house is the social center: a place for shared meals, meetings and everyday exchange. Some residents earn income away from the property; others contribute regular hours to gardening, repair, cooking, cleaning and land care.

Design posture
Observe before intervening. Repair weak connections before adding new complexity.

Three ethics, lived locally

Earth care

Conserve water, restore soil, protect habitat and prepare for wildfire.

People care

Support dignified housing, useful work, shared spaces and mutual care.

Fair share

Share tools, skills, vehicles, food and harvests within and beyond the farm.

“The strongest asset was not any one structure or garden. It was the community’s ability to share labor, knowledge and tools.”
