Original Workbook

The website is the illustrated reading edition. This 112-page workbook is the unabridged record: field observations, photographs, interviews, research, maps, changing ideas, final designs and reflections from the 2022 Quail Springs Permaculture Design Course.

A design process preserved intact

The evidence behind the atlas

This is not merely a final presentation. It shows how the design emerged through prolonged observation, conversations with residents, research, site measurements, sketches and repeated revision. Ideas that were tentative, unfinished or never implemented remain visible as part of the historical record.

Source and collaboration

A course workbook became a living archive

The design project was prepared by Robert Redecker in collaboration with Andrew Ranjbaran and the Full Circle Farm community during the Spring 2022 Quail Springs Permaculture Design Course.

The workbook itself acknowledges generations of land tenders and teachers, and credits earlier workbook formats associated with Andrew Millison and Daniel Parra-Hensel before adaptation for the Quail Springs course.

Editorial method: the web edition corrects layout, organizes themes and adds navigational context, but the downloadable PDF remains the authoritative original source.
